# Surveyor App - Govt of Tripura

## Overview
This mobile application is designed for surveyors under the Government of Tripura to efficiently conduct land surveys, capture data, and submit reports directly from the field.

## Design Goals
*   **Official & Trustworthy:** Reflects the authority of the Government of Tripura through professional aesthetics.
*   **High Utility:** Optimized for field use with clear typography, high-contrast elements, and intuitive navigation.
*   **Accessibility:** Ensures ease of use for all surveyors, regardless of technical proficiency.

## Visual Identity & Color Palette
The color scheme is designed to feel authoritative, professional, and highly legible for outdoor field use.

*   **Primary Color:** `#004D40` (Deep Teal/Green) - Used for primary actions, headers, and representing the official nature of the department.
*   **Secondary Color:** `#00695C` (Medium Teal) - Used for secondary buttons, active states, and highlights.
*   **Background:** `#F5F5F5` (Light Grey) - Provides a clean, neutral canvas that reduces glare in the field.
*   **Surface/Cards:** `#FFFFFF` (White) - Used for content containers to ensure maximum contrast.
*   **Text (Primary):** `#212121` (Near Black) - High contrast for critical data entry and reading.
*   **Text (Secondary):** `#757575` (Medium Grey) - For labels and placeholder text.
*   **Status Colors:**
    *   **Success:** `#2E7D32` (Forest Green)
    *   **Error:** `#C62828` (Dark Red)
    *   **Warning:** `#F9A825` (Amber)

## Typography
*   **Font Family:** Public Sans (or a clean sans-serif equivalent)
*   **Focus:** Maximum legibility and clear hierarchy for data-heavy forms.

## Key Screens & Flows
1.  **Login/Authentication:** Secure access for authorized surveyors.
2.  **Dashboard:** Overview of assigned tasks, pending surveys, and recent activity.
3.  **Survey List:** Filterable and searchable list of land parcels or projects requiring survey.
4.  **Data Entry Form:** Structured forms for capturing land details, measurements, and ownership info.
5.  **Map Interface:** Integrated GPS and mapping for precise location tracking and boundary marking.
6.  **Media Upload:** Capture and attach photos of the land and relevant documents.
7.  **Review & Submit:** Final validation of captured data before official submission.

## Components
*   Standardized buttons, input fields, and cards to ensure consistency and ease of interaction on mobile devices.
